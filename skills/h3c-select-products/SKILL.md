# H3C Product Selection

Write in English by default. Follow an explicit user request for another language; preserve CLI, model numbers and technical identifiers.

Read [source and verification policy](../../references/source-policy.md). Use the [comparison template](../../assets/templates/product-comparison.md) when useful.

## Workflow

- Identify requirements, complete model suffixes, host/cards, Release, media/distance, region, service term, budget and currency. Reuse supplied details and ask only for decision-critical gaps.
- Use current primary specifications and platform compatibility matrices/release notes. Matching speed, connector or wavelength is insufficient proof of interchangeability. Distinguish supported, unsupported and insufficient evidence; absence from a searched document does not automatically mean unsupported.
- Compare functions, performance test conditions, expansion, power/PoE, licenses, support and lifecycle on the same basis. Use each competitor's own documentation. Do not treat marketing claims as measurements or assume every H3C model is superior.
- Separate required, optional and unconfirmed BOM items; justify quantities and compatibility. Without a formal quotation, provide a budget model or attributed user prices, not a vendor commitment.
- For TCO, define period, currency, tax basis, CAPEX/OPEX, support/license cycles, energy and labor. Avoid double counting. Define ROI period and denominator; do not compute a percentage for zero cost or unknown benefit. Unmeasured savings are not realized benefits.

Provide a recommendation, conditions, alternatives and unresolved items at the requested depth. Apply [platform scope](../../references/platform-scope.md) to servers, storage and cloud; do not transfer Comware assumptions to other systems.

Date license, lifecycle, regional and price claims. Record quote currency, tax basis, source and validity. Expired prices are historical inputs. Use [local tools](../../references/local-tools.md) for structured line-total checks; do not sum unlike currencies or tax treatments.

For optics and PoE, check both endpoints, cards, standards, per-port limits and power configuration. If the device model, port/card, Release or exact transceiver part is missing, the compatibility conclusion is insufficient evidence, including when a direct conclusion is requested. Do not present typical third-party behavior as that conclusion; give the minimum intake and a sample test on the target as the next step. Navigate via [engineering sources](../../references/engineering-sources.md). Alias matches expand candidates, never confirmed model/Release support.

For explanations, guided operations or technical peer review, apply [adaptive engineering assistance](../../references/engineer-audiences.md). Match depth and pace to this task and the user's requests, not their employer or title. Answer simple concepts directly; require platform details only for claims or actions that depend on them.
