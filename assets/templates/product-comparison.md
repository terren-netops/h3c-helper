## Cost model, when needed
Period, currency, tax basis, purchase/implementation, recurring licenses/support, energy, labor and other costs. Show sources, formulas and assumptions. Missing prices require quotes, not zero-cost entries. Use reliable calculation tools and reproducible arithmetic.

For benefits, define the baseline, benefit period, incremental cost and ROI denominator. Unknown benefits cannot support percentages; modeled returns are not realized returns.

## Recommendation
Preferred option, rationale, applicability, alternatives and unresolved information with a follow-up check.

Record quote date/expiry, currency and tax basis. Cite license, regional, lifecycle and host compatibility evidence individually. Historical prices are not current quotations.
