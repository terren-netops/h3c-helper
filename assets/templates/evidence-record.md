# Evidence record

Assign a stable evidence ID to each decisive claim. Explain missing fields, their impact and next verification steps. Metadata matches are not source verification.

| ID/claim | Source type/title | URL/original and SHA-256 | Section/page | Model/PID/Release | Document/acquisition/expiry dates | Classification/scope | Reading and verification status | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Levels: unverified, document-verified, lab-validated, customer-environment validated. Lab/customer validation requires environment, inputs, outputs, time and acceptance evidence. Snippets, 404s and login redirects remain unverified. Hashes establish content consistency only.

Keep public, internal and customer records separate. Exclude originals/catalogs from releases. See [local tools](../../references/local-tools.md) for machine-readable metadata.
