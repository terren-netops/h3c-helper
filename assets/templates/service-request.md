# Service support materials

## Case or email draft
- Product/full model and Release; reuse known facts.
- Region, incident time/time zone, business impact and continuous/intermittent behavior.
- Reproduction conditions, recent changes and completed checks/results.
- Attachments: topology, relevant configuration, logs, photos or output; record redaction status.
- Specific questions for support.
- Serial numbers, contacts and contract IDs only where necessary and authorized.

Identify missing information with its purpose. Keep new material as a draft; do not claim sending, case creation or RMA approval. Preserve genuine existing case facts.

## Process guidance, when needed
Separate current official entry, region/service, prerequisites, steps, required materials, contractual entitlements and unresolved items.

## Training outline, when needed
Audience, objectives, duration, platform version, prerequisites, modules, exercise setup, acceptance method and reference documents. Without a lab, do not claim completed experiments.

## Case status and delivery record
Existing case ID/status evidence; draft/submission state; recipient, attachment version/hash, redaction review, authorization scope and actual receipt. After timeout, retain unknown status and inspect before retrying. A draft is not sent.

## Technical handover and observation, when needed
State the unresolved technical question, receiving engineer's next action and relevant evidence. For patch/upgrade follow-up, distinguish reported installation, verified running state, business recovery and observation under representative load. Customer engineers need actionable technical interpretation; management recipients need business status. See [audience guidance](../../references/engineer-audiences.md) and [service acceptance](../../references/service-acceptance.md).
